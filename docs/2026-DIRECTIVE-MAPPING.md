# RTSPV / BESS upgrade - rules verified 13 October 2026

This release integrates the ten user-supplied documents into design calculations, source-linked review, manufacturer reference presets and financial settlement. It also checks the regulator's published versions and the August 2026 FIT decision / September scheme clarification. Source PDFs are preserved unchanged and are not redistributed with the website. Manufacturer training material is used for factual model limits, without publishing the deck, passwords or operating instructions.

## Official verification and precedence

- [PUCSL regulatory framework](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Regulatory-Frameworks-for-RTSPV-BESS_20260731.pdf) and [October BESS standards](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BESS_Standards_SLSI-and-PUCSL.pdf) match the supplied subjects.
- [August FIT decision](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Full-Final-Decision-on-Feed-in-Tariffs-August-2026.pdf): cover effective date 25 August 2026; pp. 8-9 and Annex 3 on PDF pp. 22-24. New prosumer FIT requires valid grid clearance on/after the effective date. Clearance extensions need comparison of the original and extension-date tariff; the app requests review rather than guessing. Future clearance dates need rate re-verification.
- [Annex 2](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Annex-2-Summary-of-Approved-Feed-in-Tariffs.pdf): the newer 123 kWh/kW per 30-day total payable-export cap is applied for eligible BESS schemes. The earlier guideline's 16% plant-factor calculation and inconsistent illustrative cap are not used for settlement.
- [September 2026 scheme clarification](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PUC-EER-2026_EDL_31.pdf): two-meter physical arrangements preserve Net Metering, Net Accounting and Net Plus commercial settlement. Net-metered surplus is banked/offset as units and receives no cash FIT in this model.
- [Interconnection guideline](https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidelines-on-Interconnection-and-Operation-of-RTSPV-and-BESS-for-DLs-and-Prosumers-1.pdf): current DL/PUCSL-approved settings govern. Legacy 2025 numeric profiles are labelled as references and reports require the actual approved schedule reference.

## Calculation and review changes

- A shared `DirectiveRules` evaluator powers System Designer and the Hybrid/Utility submission gate. Failed limits and unresolved evidence block final project-pack release, including zero-export cases. Booleans alone without an evidence-pack reference do not finish review.
- The battery ratio uses manufacturer usable kWh and aggregate identical-inverter continuous kW, not surge power or nominal energy. V x Ah x DoD estimates remain review items until replaced by a declared usable-energy value. A documented >5-hour engineer exception is recorded for manual review rather than approved automatically.
- Installed BESS cycle qualification is evaluated at 80% DoD, 0.5C and at least 80% retained capacity: 6000 cycles residential / 8000 commercial-industrial. Performance-test ambient must be 25±5°C unless the authority specifies otherwise. This follows the published specification and presentation p. 8; p. 7’s simplified 25°C note does not remove the stated tolerance. Different test conditions remain unknown rather than being extrapolated.
- Manufacturer-declaration evidence explicitly covers rated/usable Ah and kWh, voltage and current limits, temperature, thermal/fire characteristics, cycles and efficiency. A missing Ah rating is not manufactured from kWh and interface voltage. Installation/BMS/fire/ESD evidence includes the SLSI workshop details; scale-appropriate fire measures follow the governing specification rather than requiring every illustrated technology. Roof-slab and >1 m above-roof supports require structural-certification evidence; BESS >50 kW requires communication hardware capability.
- PV checks distinguish cold Voc, hot/cold Vmp, operating current, short-circuit current, tracker count and actual wired module power. Manufacturer models have no automatic SLSEA/DL approval status.
- Energy flow replaces the old battery-size self-use heuristic with 48 half-hour steps on one repeated typical day per month, seasonal monthly load allocation, cyclic state of charge, PV-only charging, continuous charge/discharge limits, export restriction and battery/converter losses.
- Financial projections separate unit banking, billing-period Net Accounting import offsets before surplus sales, and gross Net Plus export. Conservative BESS cap/offset allocation is disclosed when the actual DL time-band allocation is not supplied; bulk PV-only daytime metering is not replaced by retail netting. Current import energy cost is entered from the bill / approved tariff; old retail tiers and the old universal export credit are not silently used. Self-use cannot exceed actual load. Fixed rooftop FIT is not escalated with import prices; priority income ends after its applicable period, and agreement expiry / partial final years are handled without assuming renewal. Conventional IRR is not fabricated for absent or ambiguous roots.
- Draft JSON design exports and hybrid project packs include inputs, evidence references, rule version, results, source documents and review status.

## Practical limits

This is decision support, not a substitute for independent certificate verification, site testing, utility acceptance or a signed Chartered Electrical Engineer declaration. Evidence fields record the reviewer's declarations. The typical-day AC-equivalent energy model is not a measured chronological annual simulation; it is conservative for DC-coupled capture above the AC conversion limit. Calendar-month payable caps use days/30 pro-rata estimates; actual billing cycles govern payment. Net-metering banking is modelled sequentially within each projected year with zero starting historical credit and does not reconstruct an existing ten-year credit ledger or exact retail fixed/demand charges. Power-plant FX/CCPI and escalable O&M components need a separate signed-SPPA projection rather than rooftop bands. Manufacturer compatibility depends on exact models, firmware, region and the current OEM confirmation.

## Animated learning guide

The Standards Reference opens with fourteen offline SVG lessons and 56 manual steps: energy flow, storage sizing, PV strings/roof, protection, grid/islanding, testing/handover, equipment/quality, conditions/ageing, monitoring/BMS, metering/settlement, evidence/lifecycle, cables/connectors, lightning/earthing and BESS safety/scope. Fifty reference cards and seven BESS reference rows link to the relevant lesson and step. A plain-English glossary explains common units and abbreviations. The home page has a direct learning shortcut.

Perspective drawings show rooftop panels, hybrid and battery cabinets, BMS, isolation switches, switchboards, meters, utility lines and a separate EPS board. Moving particles represent average energy-transfer direction, not electrons or instantaneous AC current. Example power budgets explicitly include assumed 96% conversion efficiency, charge/discharge limits, inverter clipping and PV curtailment. Zero export retains the utility connection and permits imports; outage and EPS examples cannot energise the disconnected utility. Return of mains supply is shown with the inverter grid port still isolated pending approved reconnection conditions. SPDs are parallel protective branches, distinct from series fuses and isolation. Billing illustrations separate import/export intervals and place conversion before AC export metering; they are functional explanations rather than universal terminal wiring diagrams.

Interactive power, storage and billing examples use separate state and stop their input/change events from invalidating real project results. Storage and billing values agree with the shared rules at the five-hour boundary and across Net Metering, Net Accounting and Net Plus. Contextual lessons also appear in System Designer, Hybrid Setup, Utility Validator and Financial Analysis. Story playback advances every eight seconds, stops at the end and pauses while the document is hidden; this duration is not a protection timing setting. Pause and manual-step controls are keyboard accessible; reduced-motion preferences disable continuous animation and story playback. A phone overview and enlarged, internally scrollable diagram avoid page overflow. These concept illustrations do not issue approvals or prescribe an operating/test procedure.

## Reference-scope accuracy audit

The register was checked against the [SLSEA supporting standards list](https://energy.gov.lk/images/soorya-bala/sri-lanka-supporting-standards%20for-solar-system.pdf), the [September 2022 service-provider guideline](https://energy.gov.lk/images/energy-management/guideline-for-solar-pv-system-installation-for-solar-providers.pdf), and the linked 2026 PUCSL framework/interconnection/BESS publications. The baseline edition is labelled explicitly. Listed Sri Lankan adoptions and later IEC editions are distinguished; apparent copied part-number inconsistencies in the supporting list are flagged for checking against the licensed publication.

- SLS 1522 covers documentation/installation/testing/commissioning; SLS 1473 covers surge protection. SLS 1553 covers module safety; SLS 1544 covers module design qualification. SLS 1637 covers DC connectors, SLS 1542 is listed against EN 50618, and SLS 1472 covers lightning protection. The older SPD and degradation/warranty labels are corrected in the associated PR/IR, inspection, report, hybrid and degradation pages.
- The register and PR/IR tool use the cited insulation table (PDF pp. 59, 83): system voltage is Voc STC × 1.25; below 120 V uses 250 V / 0.5 MΩ, 120–500 V inclusive uses 500 V / 1 MΩ, and above 500 V uses 1000 V / 1 MΩ. Missing readings or an inconsistent recorded test voltage remain REVIEW. This does not replace the accepted procedure or issue site acceptance.
- The parallel-string quick check compares (N−1) × design Isc with the actual module maximum series-fuse value (baseline section 3.1). Additional fault sources require review. Satisfying that bound is not automatic permission to omit all protection.
- Grid comparisons require entered project limits and an approved schedule reference; instantaneous voltage cannot establish timed trip, ride-through or reconnection compliance. Unsupported universal limits and qualification-to-lifetime guarantees are removed from the register. Degradation projections are planning assumptions; warranty comparison requires the actual age-specific manufacturer target.

The fifty entries are a practical source-linked register for the represented workflows, not an exhaustive reproduction of all licensed standards or a certification of any installation. Exact parts, editions, numerical clauses, product evidence and project-specific approvals remain necessary.
