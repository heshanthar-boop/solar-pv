East-West Dual-Plane Optimizer

two fixed planes, conserved DC capacity, shared or separate inverter clipping

Use this when the plant is split across two opposing or independent plane orientations. A 10 MWp plant split 50/50 is modelled as 5 MWp + 5 MWp, not 20 MWp. Automatic optimization uses a minimum 1° physical tilt and refines selected candidates locally in 1° steps. Fractional search boundaries may also be tested; the search is not exhaustive.
